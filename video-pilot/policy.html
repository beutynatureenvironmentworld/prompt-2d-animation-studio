Business Terms and Conditions

Vid Best generates digital videos from your prompts. Choose your style and duration and review the total before paying. By proceeding to checkout, you agree to these terms. Submit only material you have permission to use.

After payment is confirmed, your order enters the rendering queue. You can close your browser. Delivery time depends on video length and available processing capacity.

Before you order

Prices and checkout amounts are in USD. Start with a short, low-cost video to check the style, quality and delivery before ordering a longer one. Enter an email address you can access. We send your private download link there.

Return and Refund Policy

Each video is made to order and delivered digitally. There is no physical item to return.

We review refund requests for technical faults, including a failed render, a damaged video or a missing delivery link. Email info@vid.best from your delivery address with your order reference and a description of the issue. We verify the issue before deciding the request. Your applicable consumer rights are unaffected.

How we check a delivery

We maintain a private copy of the generated video for technical review and use private cloud storage for backups. We can check the exported file, whether our mail service accepted the delivery message and whether the download link was requested. These records alone cannot establish that you opened the email or watched the video.

Privacy Policy

We use your email address, prompt, video settings and order reference to generate your video, send the download link and answer support requests. We use payment status and transaction references to match a payment to its order. You enter your card details on the payment provider’s checkout page.

Hosting, rendering, storage, payment and email providers process the information needed for their part of your order. We use browser and network identifiers to enforce preview limits. We keep order records, delivery events and private copies of generated files to investigate technical issues and payment disputes.

For questions about your data or to request access, correction or deletion, email info@vid.best from the address you used to order. Include the order reference so we can locate your records.
