Policies and Terms

Before you order

Prices and checkout amounts are in USD. Start with a short, low-cost video to check the style, quality and delivery before ordering a longer one. Enter an email address you can access. We send your private download link there.

Technical issues and refunds

We review refund requests for technical faults, including a failed render, a damaged video or a missing delivery link. Email info@vid.best from your delivery address with your order reference and a description of the issue. We verify the issue before deciding the request. Your applicable consumer rights are unaffected.

How we check a delivery

We maintain a private copy of the generated video for technical review and use private cloud storage for backups. We can check the exported file, whether our mail service accepted the delivery message and whether the download link was requested. These records alone cannot establish that you opened the email or watched the video.
